Design Rationale (Anti-Slop)

1. The Outer Monoline Shield: Classical Greek Aegis protection distilled into architectural straight lines and precision curves. Zero motorcycle wings or animal faces.
2. The Internal Monolith 'A': Structural pylon forming an abstract capital 'A' (AEGIS) providing vertical architectural balance.
3. The Emerald Q.E.D. Diamond: Represents the mathematical proof of invariant safety (the Halmos proof square). One single, intentional accent that grounds the logo in computer science.
4. Enterprise Typography: Unbroken 'A' crossbar, wide tracking, Swiss geometric proportions. Eliminates the tacky 2012 gamer font.
